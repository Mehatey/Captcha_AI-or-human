# CAPTCHA: After Human

Ten drawing based speculative proofs told through interaction, sound, and WebGL. Each stage gives the human 15 seconds on a mode specific torn drawing surface with a velocity responsive pencil sound. After TRANSFORM, an independent prompt driven AI response draws itself on the right. Both answers then become separate 3D artifacts.

1. Human: a drawn gesture becomes procedural fingerprint contours.
2. Machine: the gesture becomes a chrome prediction trace and lines of generated code.
3. Nature: the gesture becomes the spine of a branching organism.
4. Weather: the gesture becomes glass and repeats through drifting temporal echoes.
5. Mirror: the gesture becomes reflective bilateral geometry.
6. Shadow: the gesture becomes a deep extruded silhouette.
7. Echo: a sound rhythm becomes luminous spatial echoes.
8. Swarm: the gesture becomes a living particle path.
9. Relic: the gesture becomes a weathered metallic object.
10. Recall: the human redraws the first gesture from memory before the final comparison.

Stages 1 through 9 use timed draw, AI response, transform, and inspect. Only stage 10 tests memory. During inspection, drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use a secondary drag to pan. Slow automatic motion continues when the controls are idle.

## Open

Double click `index.html`. The experience is bundled to work directly from the filesystem.

## Develop

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

After editing anything in `src`, rebuild the standalone files:

```bash
npm run build
```

The final comparison calculates a local presence signal from timing variation, corrections, stroke structure, and recall similarity. It is a design prototype, not production bot protection. A deployable architecture is outlined in `SECURITY.md`.
